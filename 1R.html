Wordpower · like

We often use like with the verbs look and sound.
  • saying two things are similar: You look like your sister.
  • saying your opinion about what you heard or read: It sounds like she had a really good holiday.
  • saying what you think will happen: It looks like it might rain – it’s very cloudy.
